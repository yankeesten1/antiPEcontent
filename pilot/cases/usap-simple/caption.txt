Same anesthesia providers. Higher rates?

That is what the FTC alleged after U.S. Anesthesia Partners bought Capitol Anesthesiology in 2018. The allegation concerns reimbursement rates, not a verified increase in every patient’s bill. The public filing redacts the rate figures; we have not estimated them.

The complaint says private-equity firm Welsh Carson created U.S. Anesthesia Partners in 2012. U.S. Anesthesia Partners denied the allegations.

Legal timeline:
• May 2024: Welsh Carson was dismissed from the federal case. USAP’s dismissal motion was denied; that was not a finding of liability.
• May 2025: a separate FTC consent order limited Welsh Carson’s USAP involvement.
• April 23, 2026: the FTC announced an agreement in principle with USAP. Final implementation and later docket events have not been independently verified here.

Historical case file through April 23, 2026. Allegations are not findings.
