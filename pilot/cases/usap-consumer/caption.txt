The FTC alleged Texans faced tens of millions in extra anesthesia costs each year.

Its complaint tied those alleged costs to U.S. Anesthesia Partners’ consolidation and other challenged conduct across Texas—not just one acquisition. The complaint says private-equity firm Welsh Carson created the business in 2012. These are allegations, not findings of liability.

Why it matters for consumers: the complaint says “patients rarely choose their anesthesia providers” (¶46). It also describes how members help fund coverage through premiums (¶65). This does not establish a specific increase in anyone’s premium or every patient’s bill.

USAP denied the allegations. Welsh Carson was dismissed from the federal case in May 2024; USAP’s dismissal motion was denied, without a finding of liability. A separate May 2025 FTC consent order limited Welsh Carson’s USAP involvement. In April 2026, the FTC announced an agreement in principle with USAP.

Historical review through April 23, 2026. Later docket events and final implementation have not been independently verified.
