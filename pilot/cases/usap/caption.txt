Texas anesthesia: a quote in the FTC’s 2023 complaint.

“Awesome! Cha-ching!” — attributed in paragraph 164 to Capitol’s vice president of operations, who became a USAP executive, discussing projected repricing revenue. This is a quotation reproduced in a pleading, not an independently verified recording.

The FTC alleged higher reimbursement rates after USAP acquired Capitol in 2018. Public rate figures are redacted; none are estimated here. The complaint states Welsh Carson created USAP in 2012.

Welsh Carson was dismissed from the federal case in 2024. In May 2025, a separate FTC consent order limited its USAP involvement. USAP denied the allegations. On April 23, 2026, the FTC announced a preliminary agreement with USAP.

Coverage ends April 23, 2026. Final implementation and later docket events have not been independently verified. Allegations are not findings.
