# Texas anesthesia: source record

**Draft for review, not approved or published.** This dated case file covers the 2023 complaint through the April 23, 2026 published update. It does not claim to establish the latest docket status or final implementation.

## The quotation

> Awesome! Cha-ching!

The FTC complaint reproduces this in paragraph 164, printed page 49, and attributes it to Capitol’s vice president of operations, who became a USAP executive. It appears in a discussion of projected repricing revenue after the Capitol acquisition. We checked the wording against the captured complaint; we did not independently authenticate the original underlying communication. Heart reads the report; this is not a recording of the executive.

## What the records establish

- The 2023 complaint states the ownership history and alleges repricing after acquisitions. The rate figures at the cited Capitol passage are redacted, so no percentage or dollar amount is supplied here.
- The May 13, 2024 court order granted Welsh Carson’s motion to dismiss and denied USAP’s motion to dismiss. It did not find USAP liable on the merits.
- The FTC finalized a separate consent order concerning Welsh Carson on May 20, 2025. It limited Welsh Carson’s involvement with USAP and required specified transaction notices.
- The April 23, 2026 joint motion expressly records USAP’s denial and describes a binding term sheet.
- The FTC’s April 23, 2026 announcement describes an agreement in principle and confidential terms. Final implementation was not independently verified in this review.

The agency index was checked October 25, 2026, but its last update was April 23. The linked stay-order PDF is unsigned and marked proposed; this draft does not treat it as a signed judicial order. See REVIEW-NOTES.md and case.json for claim-level references, source hashes and limitations.
