# A stronger first impression

The original one-line headline cards were a placeholder, not a strong finished Instagram format. The new introduction is a visual prototype for **@PEUnderScrutiny**, with a five-card carousel and an alternative 20-second vertical motion reel. Choose one to test first; they tell the same story.

## What changed

- Large, short hooks that can be read on a phone.
- High-contrast yellow, coral, mint and dark ink, with consistent branding.
- One job per card: hook, stakeholders, research questions, evidence standard, audience choice.
- A simple storefront illustration and structured visual labels, rather than a paragraph pasted onto a square.
- A 1080×1350 portrait carousel; 1080×1920 video layout with important text kept in the center area for later crop/overlay review.
- A motion version with slow zooms, fast fades and a change of scene every four seconds. It is not a narrated or character-animated documentary. No audio is included; an optional voiceover script is in the ZIP.

## Use the new files

Download `introduction-v2.zip` and unzip it. For the carousel, select `carousel-01.jpg` through `carousel-05.jpg`, in order, as **one** multi-image Instagram post. For the Reel, choose `introduction-reel.mp4`. Use `caption.txt` and review the exact assets before approval. The account is still under appeal as last reported; nothing has been scheduled or posted.

## Where content should go next

This introduction is an editorial statement, not a sourced investigation. After the style is approved, factual posts should center on a documented case or one clear mechanism, not repeat generic mission statements. They need source checks and dates before release. Do not turn a question into an unsupported accusation just because the design is more dramatic.

For the pilot, test a mix of:

1. **Carousel explainers:** a specific hook, a documented mechanism or example, what the evidence supports, why it matters, and a useful takeaway.
2. **Short Reels:** one accessible question or finding, visual changes that clarify it, readable on-screen text, optional original narration, and a source reference.
3. **Occasional single graphics:** a sourced chart, a clear definition or a documented quote with context. Not every post needs animation.

Brighter colors and video are creative choices, not a promise of traffic. Review several posts per format, compare saves/shares, follower conversion where measurable and production time, then keep the formats that earn attention without sacrificing evidence. The new export adds no model/API costs; video needs local FFmpeg. Captions, imagery and music still require the user's final review and any necessary usage rights.
